The show

Ines Dauber paints the Arno at night from the same bench below Ponte Santa Trinita, one canvas a year, always in November. This is the first time all eight have hung together.

Opening hours

Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 19:00. Thursdays until 22:00, with the lights in the hall turned down to one lamp per painting.

Entry €8, free under 18.
