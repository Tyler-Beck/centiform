The Cowherd’s Ascent

On the first warm Sunday in June, Ueli drove forty cows to the high pasture, bells tuned so that no two rang alike. The last cow would not climb until he sang the song his mother sang, and he had forgotten half of it.

Told by Rosa Wenger, Rüschegg, 1931 · cut by Hanna Lüthi

The Chalet of Seven Winters

A carpenter built a house with one window shaped like a heart, and promised it would stand seven winters. It stood seventy. On the seventh winter the village danced in front of it anyway, in case.

Told by Fritz Zbinden, Plaffeien, 1948 · cut by Hanna Lüthi

The Chamois Who Wanted the Moon

Every night the chamois climbed one rock higher, and every night the moon moved one rock further. By autumn she stood on the Gantrisch summit, and decided the view was the better prize.

Told by Marie Hostettler, Guggisberg, 1925 · cut by Beat Stucki
