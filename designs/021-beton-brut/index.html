The Pour

The hall was cast in place between March 1969 and June 1971, in lifts of 1.2 metres, against rough-sawn Douglas fir boards. Every board left its grain in the wall. You can still find the knots.

Ove Karsten wanted a room with no decoration except the way it was made. The only paint in the building is the red on the doors.

1,840seats in the main hall, none more than 34 m from the stage
2.1 smid-frequency reverberation, occupied
11,400cubic metres of concrete, poured in 212 lifts
