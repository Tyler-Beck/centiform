How to read a kanga

PindoThe border on all four sides. It frames the cloth and tells the printer where to fold.
MjiThe centre, the town. One large motif: a cashew, a coconut palm, a sun.
JinaThe name: a saying printed in a band. People choose a kanga for what it says as much as for how it looks.
Asiyesikia la mkuu huvunjika guuWho will not hear the elder breaks a leg
