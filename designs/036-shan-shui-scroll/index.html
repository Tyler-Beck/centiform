九峰云

Nine Peaks: Poems from the Qingyun Hermitage

Forty-one poems written on the walls, doors and rice jars of a mountain hermitage between 1612 and 1680, collected by the monk Huiming and translated here by Lin Shuwen.

Unroll to the left, one armʼs length at a time, as a scroll is meant to be read.

九峰

晨云

Cloud at Dawn

The mountain has taken off its face.
Only the pine at the gate remains,
and the sound of someone
splitting wood below.

Wang Sheng, written on the kitchen door, 1631

王生

过桥

Crossing the Bridge

Three planks, one of them rotten.
I cross with my eyes on the far bank,
the way the old abbot said
to read a difficult sutra.

Anonymous, cut into the bridge rail

无名

夜雨

Night Rain on the Ninth Peak

The lamp is out of oil.
I count the rain by the bucket
under the broken tile:
one drop, one breath, one drop.

Huiming, on a rice jar, 1668
