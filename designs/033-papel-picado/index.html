Barrio Las Moras presentsFiesta de la Calle Olmo

Friday 18 to Sunday 20 September

Three days on Olmo Street between the laundry and the bakery, closed to cars. Everything is free except the tamales, which are $2.50 and worth it.

Friday 18Noche

  1. 18:00Flags go upBring a ladder if you have one. Mr. Ruelas brings the long one.
  2. 19:30Son jarochoLos Primos Barragán on the laundry steps.
  3. 21:00LoteríaCards 50¢. The prize is a whole cake.

Saturday 19Día

  1. 10:00Cut-paper tableDoña Lupe teaches the chisel and the fold. 30 sheets of tissue per child.
  2. 13:00Tamales and agua frescaHorchata, jamaica, tamarindo.
  3. 16:00Bicycle paradeDecorate a bike, ride it twice around the block.

Sunday 20Cierre

  1. 11:00Band from the high schoolThey have practised all summer. Please clap.
  2. 15:00Dance on the cornerCumbia until the light goes.
  3. 19:00Flags come downTake one home. Leave one for next year.
