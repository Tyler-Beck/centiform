Terms of the Workshop

Marks

Every piece carries the square monogram of the workshop, the mark of the master, and the rose mark of the Vienna assay office.

Orders

Pieces are made to order in the sequence received. Silver takes eight to twelve weeks; furniture, four to six months.

Prices

In Kronen, for silver of 900 fineness. Gilding inside bowls and beakers at 12 Kronen extra.
