The Tonic

Liane is a bitter botanical tonic, steeped for forty days in the cellars of the Maison Verlaque at Nancy. There is no wormwood in it and no alcohol: only verbena, angelica, bitter orange, and patience.

Take a small glass before dinner, over ice, with a twist of peel.

Maison Verlaque · Nancy · 1897

LianeTonique aux plantes amères

Where to Find It

At good grocers and cafés in Nancy, Metz and Strasbourg, and by post from the Maison. A bottle of 70 cl makes about twenty-five glasses.

Order a case

Verbena

Aloysia citrodora

Picked in July above Lunéville. It gives the lemon at the front of the glass.

Angelica

Angelica archangelica

The root, dug in its second autumn. It carries the long, musky middle.

Bitter Orange

Citrus aurantium

Peel only, dried in the sun at Menton. It closes the glass, dry and long.
