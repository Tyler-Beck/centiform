Every job begins with one JOB card. It names the job, says who pays for it, and tells the operator how urgent it is. The machine reads it before anything else in the deck, and will not start a job without one.

Columns 1 and 2 must hold two slashes. The job name starts in column 3 and may be up to eight characters. Leave column 11 blank. Keypunch operators: set the program drum so that column 72 stops you.

An EXEC card runs one program. Each DD card after it connects the program to one file: a tape on drive 2, a disk pack, the card reader, or the printer. A program that asks for a file with no DD card ends with code S013.

Tape mounts after 18:00 need a signed request at the window. Do not write VOL=SER=SCR001 unless you mean a scratch tape: it will be overwritten.

This is the payroll deck for the Kingsbury plant as it goes into the reader, top card first. Cards with a red stripe are control cards; plain cards are data. Hover a card to lift it from the deck.

The printer returns your deck’s output on 14⅞-inch greenbar paper. The bands are there so your eye stays on one line across 132 columns. Condition codes above 4 are printed in red on printers with a two-colour ribbon.
