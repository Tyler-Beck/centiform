The Long Way West

Two thousand nine hundred and fourteen miles, eleven days, one station wagon with a cassette deck. Our road log, sign by sign, from the Atlantic to the other ocean.

EXIT 1
Days 1–3
Coast to Plains
Miles0–812

We left Port Amity at 5:40 in the morning with the ferry horn going. By Bluecreek the hills had flattened; by Harlan Falls there was nothing but sky and grain elevators, every one of them with a town name painted on it in letters taller than the car.

Best pie: Dot’s Grill, exit 212, rhubarb, $3.25 a slice.

High Desert
Miles813–1,977
Scenic Overlook
Painted Mesa1 mi

Past mile 900 the road climbs in long, dead-straight grades and the signs start warning about wind. We counted forty-one miles between one gas station and the next, and filled up at both.

At the Painted Mesa overlook a ranger explained that the stripes in the rock are 180 million years of sea floor. The kids asked if there were fish in it. There are.

End of the Road

The last sign on the interstate reads END in white on green. After it, a stop light, a bait shop, and the ocean. We parked, walked into the water to our knees, and drove back to the motel to find sand in the cassette deck.
