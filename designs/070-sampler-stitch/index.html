The Wexford Needlework Guild

Annual Show of Samplers

Two hundred and twelve pieces by forty-six members, from a first alphabet worked by Aoife, aged seven, to Mrs Delia Byrne’s map of the county in 11,000 stitches.

The sampler on the left was worked by the whole guild, one row each, on 28-count Irish linen in silks from the Dublin Road dye house.
