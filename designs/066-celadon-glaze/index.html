Celadon from Icheon

Inlaid stoneware in the Goryeo manner, thrown and carved by Yoon Seo-hee and fired twice in a wood kiln.

The Glaze

A little iron in the ash glaze, fired in reduction at 1,250 °C. Too much air and it turns olive; too little and it greys.
