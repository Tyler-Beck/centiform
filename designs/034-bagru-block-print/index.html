Bagru Chhapa Sangh

Eleven Chhipa printing families on one street in Bagru, forty minutes from Jaipur. We print by hand with teak blocks carved by Ramesh Kumawat, and we dye with what grows or rusts nearby.

  1. HardaUnbleached cotton soaked in myrobalan. It turns the cloth this warm yellow and takes the black.
  2. Syahi and begarBlack from old iron and jaggery, red from alum that later meets madder in the pot.
  3. DabuMud, gum and lime pressed on by block. Wherever it sits, the indigo cannot reach.
  4. Indigo vatDipped, lifted, oxidised green to blue. Four dips for mid-blue, nine for night.
  5. Sun and riverDried on the sand by the pond. Washed until the water runs clear.
