Instructions

  1. Insert coin. 25¢ one play, 50¢ three plays.
  2. Hit all five COMET drop targets to light the pop bumpers for 1,000.
  3. Complete Q-U-E-E-N rollovers to light the SPECIAL on the left outlane.
  4. Crown saucer scores 5,000 and advances the bonus multiplier to 5×.
  5. Tilt ends the ball in play. Matching the last two digits scores a free game.
