Five kinds, one season

My great-grandfather Vicent Soler planted the first Navels in 1934, on land his father had used for rice. We still pick those rows first.

We grow for flavour, not for shelf life, so the fruit arrives with its leaves on and a little green at the stalk. Keep it cool and eat it within three weeks.

Amparo Soler, fourth generation

When to order

Darker means better. We open each variety for orders when the sugar reads above 11 °Brix, and close it when the trees are bare.
