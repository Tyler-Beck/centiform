The Halvorsen Slides Duluth, Minn., 1958–1971

Eleven yellow boxes from the hall closet, 412 mounts, sorted by year onto Grandpa Arne's light table. Each slide keeps the lab's date stamp and Mom's grease-pencil note. Pick a tray to lay it out; hover to look through the loupe.
