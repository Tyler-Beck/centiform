Route 7 · The Coast

Between Tirsa and Old Varan the shelters face the sea. Most were designed by Irena Stolz, who worked from a kitchen table in Tirsa and never saw a shelter built except her own street’s.

The Wave, Sailor’s Rest

A single folded shell, 90 mm thick at the lip. The mosaic on the back wall is a school of mackerel in 11 blues.

Built
1971
Design
I. Stolz

The Gull, Cape Orlo

Two cantilevered wings on one pier. Children call it the aeroplane. It shelters nobody from rain that comes sideways, which is all the rain on Cape Orlo.

Built
1968
Design
Roads Office, drawing 68/14

The Drum, Varan Cannery

A concrete cylinder with two portholes, for the cannery shift bus. The women from the night shift paid for the mosaic sun themselves in 1979.

Route 12 · The Pass

Above the tree line the shelters had to carry snow. They are heavier, lower, and several have a stove flue that no longer has a stove.

The Arch, Dolna Mill

A parabola poured in one day in October 1966, before the first frost. The inside is lined with a red-and-gold field of wheat.

Built
1966
Design
P. Mares
Smalti
c. 30,000 pieces

The Stack, Upper Kesh
