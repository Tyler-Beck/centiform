Letter to Aunt Vera

Dear Aunt Vera,

I am writing this on the new Lisle 128 that Dad brought home from the office for the weekend. It has a picture on the screen of a real sheet of paper, and a little box you push around the desk called a mouse.

The letters come out exactly as they will print. I can make them underlined, outlined or with a shadow, from the Style menu. There are no colours, only black and white, but the grey bits are made of tiny dots, like newspaper photos.

We saved this letter on a disk the size of a coaster. It holds 400K. Dad says that is more than enough for everything he will ever write.

Love from Pip
Saturday 14 April 1984
