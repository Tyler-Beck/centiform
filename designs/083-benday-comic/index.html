Feature · Marisol Keane

Meridian, sixty-four years on

In the autumn of 1962 a nine-cent book with a misregistered cover landed on the spinner rack at Mercer Street. The red plate sat three millimetres left of the black, so Captain Meridian’s cape floated beside him like a shadow. Readers loved it. The publisher fixed it in issue two and sales fell by a third.

This week’s facsimile keeps the error. It is printed on uncoated stock, four colours, with the dot screens shot from an original copy lent by a regular, Walt Dobrowski, who bought it new at the age of eleven for pocket money and a returned bottle.

Walt will be in the shop on Saturday from noon with the original in its bag and board. Look, do not touch. He means it.

Saturday 26th, noon till four! Dolores Achebe signs Harbour Witch. Two books per person, no slabs, please!
