1preliminary course

every student begins here, for six months, whatever they came to study.

you will cut paper, fold wire, glue matchsticks and balance stones. you will draw one circle a thousand times. by march you will know what a material wants to do, and what it refuses.

there is no examination. at the end of the course the masters look at what you made and tell you which workshop will have you.

2the workshops

metalteapots, lamps, door handles. a lathe, two forges, one silversmith.
weavingfourteen looms. cloth for the theatre, for chairs, for sale.
furnituretubular steel since spring. the chairs in the canteen came from here.
printingone press, sans-serif only, and no capital letters.
wall paintingcolour plans for the housing estate at kiefernweg.
stagethe triangle ballet, performed each june on the roof.

3the masters

eleven masters of form and seven masters of craft teach in pairs: one who knows why, one who knows how. they live in the three double houses behind the pines and take their lunch in the canteen with you.

the director, ernst vollrath, teaches architecture to the third year and keeps his door open on thursdays from four until six.

4admission

send ten pieces of your own work, a short letter about yourself in your own hand, and a photograph. no school certificate is needed. women and men are admitted on the same terms.

applications close on 1 september. we answer every letter within three weeks.
