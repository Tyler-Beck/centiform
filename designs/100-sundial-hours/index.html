This page keeps your time. Its paper warms at dawn, pales at noon, goes copper at six and blue at night, and the gnomon’s shadow crosses it as the sun would cross our garden dial.

XII

Noon mark

12:00 to 14:00

At local apparent noon, a brass line on the floor of the Long Room is lit by a pinhole in the roof. It arrives up to sixteen minutes off the clock. We explain why.
