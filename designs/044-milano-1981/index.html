Collettivo Nebbia presentsMilano 1981

Twelve pieces, no rules. First shown on 18 September 1981 in a gallery on Corso Garibaldi, to a crowd of 2,000 and a very worried landlord.

Plastic laminate, lacquered wood, cast glass and neon, made in small runs by workshops in Brianza.
