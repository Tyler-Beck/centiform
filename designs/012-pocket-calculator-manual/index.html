Caution

  • Do not leave the calculator in a car in summer. The display may turn black above 60 °C.
  • Do not press the display. It is glass.
  • Clean with a soft, dry cloth. Never use thinner or benzine.

Memory, in short

When a number is in memory, a small M shows at the top left of the display. Press MR twice to clear the memory.
