The Summer Bill

Nine nights in the old Masonic dance hall on Geary, from July to the end of August. Doors at 8:30. Dancing encouraged. Chairs discouraged.

The light show runs from four overhead projectors and a bowl of coloured oil.

  1. Jul 7–9The Lantern Mice, Sister Obsidian, Big Weather$3.50
  2. Jul 21–23Coyote Radio, The Glass Harmonica Band$3.00
  3. Aug 4–6Mother Fern, Quicksilver Hymnal$3.50
  4. Aug 25–27Closing: all six bands, till dawn$5.00
