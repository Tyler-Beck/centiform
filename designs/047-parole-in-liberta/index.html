Manifestoof Electric Speed

Ottavio Brenna · Milan · 11 February 1913 · price 10 centesimi

Thesis 1

We declare that the street is faster than the museum. A tram at full current is more beautiful than a thousand painted saints standing still.

Thesis 2

We will set words free from the line. A word that shouts will be large; a word that whispers will be small; a word that runs will lean.

Thesis 3

Punctuation is abolished, except the exclamation mark, which we will use as a hammer, and the plus sign, which joins one engine to the next.

Thesis 4

Every Sunday at noon, from the steps of the Galleria, we will read this manifesto aloud while a motorcycle runs its engine beside us. Come early; bring cotton for your ears.
